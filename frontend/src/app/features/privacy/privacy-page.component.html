Privacy

This site counts its own visits, so I can see which projects people look at. It does that without cookies and without any third-party service.

What is counted

  • Which pages are viewed: the About page, the list of projects, each project's page, the contact page and this one.
  • Clicks on a project's own links, such as its repository.

How

To count each visitor once a day, your IP address is combined with a random key that is replaced every day and never saved. If your address is IPv6, only its first half is used: the part that names your network, so the devices on one home or office network count as one visitor. Only the result is stored. Once that day's key is gone, the result cannot be traced back to your address. The counting does not store your IP address, and nothing is saved in your browser.

What is not collected

No cookies, no fingerprinting and no third-party analytics. The page you came from, your browser and your device are not recorded. The time of a visit is kept only as the day.

Opting out

If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, nothing is counted.

The contact form

If you send a message, your name, email address and message are stored so I can reply. Your IP address is not stored. To limit spam, the server keeps a count of recent messages per address in memory only, and loses it when it restarts.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Netlify, and its API runs on a server behind Cloudflare. Like any web host, they may keep their own logs of requests.
